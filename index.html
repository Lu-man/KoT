A little about us

Built on experience.
Driven by the fix.

KOT Automotive Engineering (Pty) Ltd was founded by Shawn Wayne Le Roux in March 2014, with a clear ambition: make specialist thread repair more convenient, reliable and accessible.

With 15 years of threading-industry experience at the time of founding, Shawn set out to bring pioneering bolt-extraction and thread-cutting techniques directly to customers. Today, our highly trained technicians and accountable management team deliver quality workmanship and cost-effective solutions.

We support customers across the automotive, commercial, civil, mining, aviation, rail and marine sectors, helping them keep their own service commitments on track.
